Introduction

A Boutique Built Around You.

Sartaj Alam Designing Boutique is located at 17/44, Zakir Nagar, New Friends Colony, New Delhi. Here, clothing is designed around the person wearing it — from Indian suits and dresses to contemporary Western styles.

Every garment begins with a conversation — your occasion, your reference, your comfort. Measurements are taken according to the required garment, and the piece is cut and stitched according to your requirements.

Women from Zakir Nagar, Jamia Nagar, New Friends Colony and nearby South Delhi areas visit us for custom stitching, personalised fitting, festive looks and everyday elegance. Rated 5.0 ★ by customers on Google.
